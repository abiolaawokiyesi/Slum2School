Deduction rates
Set the rates the payroll engine applies to gross pay. These are organisation-wide. Enter your own S2S-confirmed figures.
Note: each rate is applied as a flat percentage of gross. Nigerian PAYE is actually banded (graduated) and pension is normally on basic+housing+transport, not full gross — confirm with Finance before relying on these figures for live pay.
